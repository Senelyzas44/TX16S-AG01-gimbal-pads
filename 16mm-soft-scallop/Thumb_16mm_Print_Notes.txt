THUMB 16 — FDM prototype, units mm
Print two identical copies for left and right sticks.

DESIGN
16 mm maximum pad diameter, 11 mm stem diameter, 15.2 mm body height.
Shallow 0.8 mm bowl, six smoothly scalloped edges, six broad radial ribs.
Ribs peak at 0.4 mm relief with approximately 1.2 mm width at half height.
Smooth central contact region. Sloping underside and rounded rim.
Installed height depends on shaft engagement, NOT body height alone.

UNCONFIRMED MOUNT — READ BEFORE PRINTING
Retains the previous assumed M4 insert interface, NOT a measurement of your hardware.
Intended insert specification from the previous design: ruthex RX-M4x8.1.
Pocket: 5.6 mm diameter; 9.2 mm total depth from base; 0.4 mm entry chamfer.
Shaft-tip relief: 4.4 mm diameter, from z=9.2 to 11.8 mm.
The STL contains a smooth heat-set pocket, NOT a printed M4 thread.
Do not substitute a different insert just because its internal thread is M4.
Verify the insert maker's pilot diameter, external diameter and length.
Print the supplied coupon in the same material/settings and test your insert there.
If it does not fit securely, stop and provide insert measurements for revision.
The coupon reproduces the pocket and blind roof, not the thumb pad.

PRINT
PETG suggested; 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.12–0.16 mm layers, 5 walls, 100% infill.
Flat stem base on bed, pad upward. No external supports intended.
Inspect the internal blind roof/4.4 mm bridge in slicer; tune bridging as needed.
Avoid supports inside the blind pocket, where removal is difficult.
Place seam away from the main thumb-contact region; remove sharp burrs gently.
Install heat-set insert from the underside, square and flush, using controlled heat
per its manufacturer. Never install it while the stick is on the transmitter.
Allow it to cool completely; reject cracked, distorted or loose parts.

BEFORE USE
Confirm shaft thread independently. Do not force the assembly or bottom out the shaft.
Check adequate thread engagement and secure attachment using your transmitter's
appropriate retention method. Confirm full travel in every direction with no contact
against the gimbal surround, switches or case. Test comfort and retention on the bench
with the aircraft powered off before flying. No physical print or load tests performed.
